Questions to ask about scheduling and travel

What to prepare for your estimate

A few minutes of prep makes the first visit much more useful.

  • Your address and which town you're in, Grovetown or Harlem
  • Photos of the area you want changed
  • A rough idea of the size and what you'd like to see there
  • Any spots where water pools or soil washes away
  • Notes on pets, kids or gate access that affect how the crew works

If the project involves digging, such as a wall footing or a fire pit area, remember that you should call 811 before any ground is broken so buried lines get marked.

Mistakes people make with a local landscaping company

Waiting for the weekend to ask for a quote. We're closed then, so you'll lose a few days. Call early in the week.

Describing the job too loosely. "Make it look nice" is hard to price. Say what you want: a paver walkway, a retaining wall on the slope, a fire pit near the back patio.

Leaving out the lawn. A new patio can wreck turf around it. Ask how the surrounding grass will be repaired, and whether sod or aeration makes sense afterward.

Picking a spot for a fire pit without checking clearance. Keep it away from fences, trees and the house, and talk it through with the crew on site.
